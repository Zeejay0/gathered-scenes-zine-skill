# Adaptive Flat Tear Boundary v0.2

Use one scene-aware global path and let a broad high-amplitude low-frequency field create the visible torn-paper silhouette.

## Accepted boundary

- Preserve one legible scene-aware direction or guide curve.
- Keep the preset path simple; use only 3–7 points for a materially source-derived custom curve.
- Apply broad, visibly readable low-frequency rises and falls to the tear boundary.
- Keep turns rounded and non-periodic, with small unequal notches and changing opening width.
- Add a narrow warm fiber fringe without introducing large debris or depth.

## Rejected contour

- A straight or uniformly smooth digital wipe.
- Three to six large, widely spaced zigzags.
- Repeating triangles, equal angles, equal spacing, or a regular waveform.
- Needle-sharp spikes, deep mechanical cuts, or uniformly noisy fuzz.
- Large detached debris, curling sheets, lifted corners, or 3D shadows.

## Division of responsibility

The agent selects only the global direction or a 3–7 point composition-aware path. The renderer owns the broad low-frequency contour variation and fiber detail. Do not manually encode local teeth in `--path-points`.

Keep the accepted 3.0-second timing and frozen-poster invariant unchanged.
